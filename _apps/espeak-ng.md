## Make it your phone’s voice

1. Open eSpeak NG once and wait for the voices to finish setting up.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Accessibility**, then **Text-to-speech output**. On Samsung phones this is under **General management**.
3. Under **Preferred engine**, choose **eSpeak NG**.
4. Pick your language and speech rate in the eSpeak NG app.

TalkBack and other screen readers will now use eSpeak NG.
