## Make it your phone's voice

1. Open Gemini TTS once and choose a voice.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Accessibility**, then **Text-to-speech output**. On Samsung phones this is under **General management**.
3. Under **Preferred engine**, choose **Gemini TTS**.

The GenAI voices are only on phones Google supports, such as the Galaxy S25 series. On other phones, Gemini TTS uses Google's standard voices.
